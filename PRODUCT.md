Tokyo-based AI research, engineering, and consulting company. Audience: enterprise business sponsors and technical decision-makers. Primary action: qualified project inquiry.

The website connects model expertise with agents, applications, and secure deployment. Factual company information comes from the existing repository and AGENTS.md. Do not invent clients, outcomes, benchmarks, credentials, partnerships, or response-time guarantees. Model adaptation depends on access, licensing, data, and compute.

English routes remain unprefixed; Japanese routes use /ja. Both are server rendered with equivalent content. Repository-managed research entries distinguish directions, illustrative artifacts, and actual findings. The first artifact is a deterministic synthetic purchase-request workflow, not a model benchmark or client engagement.

Contact keeps the existing /api/contact contract and mail configuration. Careers retains /career and /api/application. Deployment is outside this implementation scope.
